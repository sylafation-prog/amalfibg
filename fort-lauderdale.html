On the ground

What we hold in Fort Lauderdale

Amalfi Group serves commercial property, restaurants and waterfront residences throughout Fort Lauderdale and Broward County. Dispatch runs at every hour, with a four-hour emergency target in the county, and every engagement begins with a survey at no cost.

Downtown office towers

Las Olas Boulevard and the Flagler Village edge carry 1980s and 1990s towers on central chiller plants. Most are at or past the point where tube cleaning and retrofit decisions matter more than another patch.

The beach corridor

Hotels and condominiums along A1A sit in the worst salt exposure in Broward. Condenser coils here fail years earlier than they do two miles inland, which is why our coastal programme runs on a tighter interval east of the Intracoastal.

Las Olas and Himmarshee restaurants

Kitchen makeup air, grease waste and walk-in refrigeration, on properties that cannot close for a repair. Most of our restaurant work in the county is within a mile of these two streets.

The Isles and Rio Vista

Waterfront houses with docks, lifts, seawalls and pools — and the paint, finish and corrosion cycle that comes with a canal at the end of the garden.

Questions

What Fort Lauderdale owners ask first

How quickly do you respond to an emergency in Fort Lauderdale?

Four hours, day or night. Dispatch runs at every hour, and clients under an agreement are attended first and pay no overtime premium. Targets for every county are set out on the coverage page.

Do you charge for the survey in Fort Lauderdale?

No. Every engagement begins with a survey at no cost, and the written report is yours whether or not you engage us. For a commercial building that means an equipment inventory — make, model, serial, age, tonnage, refrigerant and condition of every unit, with a repair-or-replace list in priority order. For a residence it is a condition walk of the exterior, the systems and the waterfront structures.

What areas of Fort Lauderdale do you cover?

Las Olas, Downtown, Flagler Village, Victoria Park, Rio Vista, Harbor Beach, Las Olas Isles, Coral Ridge, Lauderdale Beach, Sailboat Bend, Colee Hammock, Poinsettia Heights. We cover Broward County entirely, so if a neighbourhood is not named here, tell us the address.

Do you work on both commercial property and private homes in Fort Lauderdale?

Yes, through separate divisions with separate crews. Amalfi Mechanical covers commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical. Amalfi Estates covers year-round care of waterfront residences. Amalfi Construction covers tenant buildout and renovation. A hotel's chief engineer and a family at home need entirely different things, so the two are never interchangeable.
