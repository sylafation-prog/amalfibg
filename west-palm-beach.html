What we hold in West Palm Beach

Amalfi Group serves downtown commercial property and island estates throughout West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County. Dispatch runs at every hour, with a four-hour emergency target in the county, and every engagement begins with a survey at no cost.

Downtown and Clematis

Office towers, restaurant row and a growing inventory of new mixed-use. The newer buildings are on modern VRF and controls; the older ones are not, and the two need very different attention.

Rosemary Square and the retail core

Multi-tenant retail and restaurant with shared plant, where a landlord wants a single point of accountability across the whole property.

Palm Beach island

Estates with the exposure of an exposed barrier island — salt from both sides, seasonal occupancy, and owners who expect a property to be perfect on the day they arrive.

Northwood and the medical corridor

Clinical and professional space where humidity control and filtration matter more than comfort, and where downtime is not an option.

Local conditions

Seasonal properties

A house or a club that sits closed for six months needs more attention, not less. Humidity does more damage to an unoccupied Florida property than occupancy ever does. Our house watch and dehumidification programmes exist because of exactly this.

Questions

What West Palm Beach owners ask first

How quickly do you respond to an emergency in West Palm Beach?

Four hours, day or night. Dispatch runs at every hour, and clients under an agreement are attended first and pay no overtime premium. Targets for every county are set out on the coverage page.

Do you charge for the survey in West Palm Beach?

No. Every engagement begins with a survey at no cost, and the written report is yours whether or not you engage us. For a commercial building that means an equipment inventory — make, model, serial, age, tonnage, refrigerant and condition of every unit, with a repair-or-replace list in priority order. For a residence it is a condition walk of the exterior, the systems and the waterfront structures.

What areas of West Palm Beach do you cover?

Clematis Street, Rosemary Square, Downtown, Northwood, El Cid, Flamingo Park, SoSo, Palm Beach Island, Singer Island, Palm Beach Lakes. We cover Palm Beach County entirely, so if a neighbourhood is not named here, tell us the address.

Do you work on both commercial property and private homes in West Palm Beach?

Yes, through separate divisions with separate crews. Amalfi Mechanical covers commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical. Amalfi Estates covers year-round care of waterfront residences. Amalfi Construction covers tenant buildout and renovation. A hotel's chief engineer and a family at home need entirely different things, so the two are never interchangeable.
