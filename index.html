Our standard

Finished means finished

Amalfi was founded by a European-born operator who spent twenty years owning and managing commercial buildings, and renovating them with his own hands. The standard follows from that: a job is done when the edges are right, not when the invoice is ready.

Written before it begins

Every property receives an assessment and a schedule in writing before a crew is sent. You know what is to be done, when, and at what cost. Changes are agreed in advance, never discovered afterwards.

The same faces each time

One named steward to a property. The people who arrive know where the shutoffs are, which unit failed last August and which gate code works. Continuity is the service.

Shown when it is done

Photographs and a written condition report after each visit, sent to your telephone. You see the work whether or not you were on the property that day.

The European standard

Specified for fifty years, not for the warranty period

In Europe a tradesman qualifies under a master before he is allowed to work unsupervised, tile and stone are set to tolerances written in millimetres, and a building is detailed on the assumption that somebody will still be maintaining it in fifty years rather than replacing it in fifteen. That is the habit we build with here.

It is not an affectation on this coast. The Amalfi Coast and South Florida face the same three adversaries — salt, hard sun and masonry that moves — and one of them has been answering them for several centuries longer. What it means on your property is a tighter tolerance, a longer-lived specification, and details built to be opened and serviced instead of torn out.
