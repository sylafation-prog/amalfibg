Questions

What Delray Beach owners ask first

How quickly do you respond to an emergency in Delray Beach?

Four hours, day or night. Dispatch runs at every hour, and clients under an agreement are attended first and pay no overtime premium. Targets for every county are set out on the coverage page.

Do you charge for the survey in Delray Beach?

No. Every engagement begins with a survey at no cost, and the written report is yours whether or not you engage us. For a commercial building that means an equipment inventory — make, model, serial, age, tonnage, refrigerant and condition of every unit, with a repair-or-replace list in priority order. For a residence it is a condition walk of the exterior, the systems and the waterfront structures.

What areas of Delray Beach do you cover?

Atlantic Avenue, Pineapple Grove, Downtown Delray, Delray Beach oceanfront, Lake Ida, Tropic Isle, Seagate, Marina District. We cover Palm Beach County entirely, so if a neighbourhood is not named here, tell us the address.

Do you work on both commercial property and private homes in Delray Beach?

Yes, through separate divisions with separate crews. Amalfi Mechanical covers commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical. Amalfi Estates covers year-round care of waterfront residences. Amalfi Construction covers tenant buildout and renovation. A hotel's chief engineer and a family at home need entirely different things, so the two are never interchangeable.
