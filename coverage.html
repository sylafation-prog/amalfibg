Where we serve

Both coasts, south of the line

Everything south of West Palm Beach on the Atlantic and south of Tampa on the Gulf. Crews are sent from the nearest county, never from one distant office — the drive is the part of the job nobody should be paying for.

The nine counties

County by county

Amalfi Group covers nine Florida counties: Palm Beach, Broward, Miami-Dade and Monroe on the Atlantic, and Manatee, Sarasota, Charlotte, Lee and Collier on the Gulf. The towns below are the ones we attend most often — the coverage is the whole county in each case.

What runs in each county

All three divisions, everywhere

The service area is the same for all three divisions. Amalfi Mechanical holds commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical for hotels, restaurants, retail centres and office property. Amalfi Estates keeps waterfront residences on a written calendar of care. Amalfi Construction takes tenant buildout, renovation and remodel.

Ten cities have a page of their own, describing the building stock and the local conditions there — Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Miami Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach, Delray Beach, Pompano Beach, Naples, Fort Myers and Sarasota.
