On the ground

What we hold in Miami Beach

Amalfi Building Group serves hotels, restaurants and condominium common areas throughout Miami Beach and Miami-Dade County. Dispatch runs at every hour, with a four-hour emergency target in the county, and every engagement begins with a survey at no cost.

The Art Deco district

Hotels from the 1930s and 1940s running systems added decades later into structures that never anticipated them. Access is the whole problem — equipment often has to be craned, and the historic preservation board has a view on what can be seen from the street.

Collins Avenue oceanfront

Full-service hotels on central plant, with the most aggressive salt exposure of any property type we serve. Coil coating here is not an upsell, it is the only thing that gets a condenser past year seven.

South Beach restaurants

Tight kitchens, no storage, no closing. Refrigeration failures here are same-day problems and the back-of-house access is usually an alley at 6am.

Mid-Beach and North Beach condominiums

Towers from the 1960s and 1970s, many now facing milestone inspection and reserve funding alongside ageing common-area mechanical.

Local conditions

Salt is the adversary here

Chloride drives into condenser coils and eats fins from the inside. On the beach we clean quarterly and coat every two years, because the alternative is replacing a rooftop unit at year eight instead of year seventeen. It is the single highest-return line in any Miami Beach maintenance agreement.

Questions

What Miami Beach owners ask first

How quickly do you respond to an emergency in Miami Beach?

Four hours, day or night. Dispatch runs at every hour, and clients under an agreement are attended first and pay no overtime premium. Targets for every county are set out on the coverage page.

Do you charge for the survey in Miami Beach?

No. Every engagement begins with a survey at no cost, and the written report is yours whether or not you engage us. For a commercial building that means an equipment inventory — make, model, serial, age, tonnage, refrigerant and condition of every unit, with a repair-or-replace list in priority order. For a residence it is a condition walk of the exterior, the systems and the waterfront structures.

What areas of Miami Beach do you cover?

South Beach, Ocean Drive, Collins Avenue, Lincoln Road, Sunset Harbour, Mid-Beach, Faena District, North Beach, Normandy Isle, Venetian Islands, Star, Palm and Hibiscus Islands. We cover Miami-Dade County entirely, so if a neighbourhood is not named here, tell us the address.

Do you work on both commercial property and private homes in Miami Beach?

Yes, through separate divisions with separate crews. Amalfi Mechanical covers commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical. Amalfi Estates covers year-round care of waterfront residences. Amalfi Construction covers tenant buildout and renovation. A hotel's chief engineer and a family at home need entirely different things, so the two are never interchangeable.
