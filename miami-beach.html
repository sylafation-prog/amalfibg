What we hold in Miami Beach

The Art Deco district

Hotels from the 1930s and 1940s running systems added decades later into structures that never anticipated them. Access is the whole problem — equipment often has to be craned, and the historic preservation board has a view on what can be seen from the street.

Collins Avenue oceanfront

Full-service hotels on central plant, with the most aggressive salt exposure of any property type we serve. Coil coating here is not an upsell, it is the only thing that gets a condenser past year seven.

South Beach restaurants

Tight kitchens, no storage, no closing. Refrigeration failures here are same-day problems and the back-of-house access is usually an alley at 6am.

Mid-Beach and North Beach condominiums

Towers from the 1960s and 1970s, many now facing milestone inspection and reserve funding alongside ageing common-area mechanical.
