The house

Finished means finished.

Amalfi was founded by a European-born operator who spent twenty years owning and managing commercial buildings, and renovating them with his own hands rather than hiring someone to hold the schedule for him.

Why this exists

From the other side of the table

Most contractors have never been the client. They have not waited three days for a call back about a unit that failed on a Friday, or paid for the same repair twice because nobody wrote down what was done the first time, or discovered a change order on an invoice rather than before the work.

We have. Amalfi was built by someone who spent two decades on the owner's side — budgeting for equipment replacement, chasing vendors who did not arrive, and running his own renovations because the alternative was worse. Every commitment on this page exists because its absence cost us something.

That is also why the three divisions sit under one roof. A property does not experience its problems by trade. It experiences them as a list of people who need to be called, and nobody who is answerable for the whole of it.

Three commitments

What you can hold us to

Written before it begins

Every property receives an assessment and a schedule in writing before a crew is sent. You know what is to be done, when, and at what cost. Changes are agreed in advance, never discovered afterwards.

The same faces each time

One named steward to a property. The people who arrive know where the shutoffs are, which unit failed last August and which gate code works. Continuity is not a courtesy — it is the service.

Shown when it is done

Photographs and a written condition report after each visit, sent to your telephone. You see the work whether or not you were on the property that day, and the record accumulates into a history of the building.

The way we work

Particulars

On site

  • Background checks on every person who attends a property
  • Uniformed crews in marked vehicles
  • A named lead per property, not a rotation
  • Certificates of insurance before the first visit
  • Licensed specialists on every permitted portion

On paper

  • A written scope with exclusions stated plainly
  • Change orders priced and signed before the work
  • Lien waivers collected at every payment
  • One statement, itemised, with no hidden margin
  • A closeout file you keep

Craft

A European standard of finish

We talk about edges, tolerances, surface preparation and how many coats because those are the things that decide whether work lasts two years or seven. On this coast, where salt and sun shorten every cycle, preparation is not a detail. It is the whole job.

Amalfi is a Florida company. The standard is the founder's; the crews, the licences and the accountability are local.
