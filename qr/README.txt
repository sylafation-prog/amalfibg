AMALFI — QR ARTWORK
===================

VEHICLE WRAP
  File:      amalfi-van-forest.pdf   (vector, 8 in square, forest #2A3C30, transparent)
             amalfi-van-forest.eps   (same, EPS)
             amalfi-van-forest.svg   (same, SVG)
             amalfi-van-cream.svg    (cream #F4F1E8 modules, for placing on a forest panel)
  Encodes:   https://amalfibg.com/van
  Error correction: H (30%)

  PRINT SIZE — 8 inches square minimum, including the clear border.
  Scan distance is roughly ten times the printed width, so 8 in reads from
  about 6 feet. Do not print below 6 in.

  PLACEMENT
    - Flat panel only. Not across a body line, door seam, handle or corrugation.
    - Lower rear quarter or the rear doors. People scan a parked van from the
      pavement, at roughly waist to chest height.
    - Keep the full quiet zone. Nothing — no type, no rule, no graphic — inside
      the blank border built into the file.
    - Matte or satin laminate. Gloss throws glare and kills the scan.
    - Do not recolour, rotate, stretch, round the corners, or drop a logo in
      the middle.

  CONTRAST
    Forest on cream is correct. If it must sit on a dark panel, use the cream
    version on forest. Never mid-tone on mid-tone.
