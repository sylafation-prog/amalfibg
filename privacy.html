Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not

A short, plain account of what happens when you read these pages, send us an enquiry, or use the client portal. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@amalfibg.com and we will answer it in plain words.

In effect 7 November 2026

Who we are

Amalfi Building Group LLC, Fort Lauderdale, Florida. This policy covers amalfibg.com, the client portal at portal.amalfibg.com, and the email address published on both. Nothing is sold through this website, and no payment is taken anywhere on it.

Reading the site

What is collected if you only read

Three measurement tools, used to understand which pages are read and which lead to enquiries. None of them is an advertising tool, and none of them is used to follow you to other websites.

ToolWhat it recordsCookies
Google Analytics 4 Pages viewed, how you arrived, approximate city derived from your network address, device and browser type. Yes
Microsoft Clarity How the page is used — scrolling, mouse movement and clicks — including a replay of the session. See below. Yes
Cloudflare Web Analytics Page views and load performance only, counted without identifying the visitor. No

Our host, Cloudflare, also keeps ordinary server logs that include network addresses, as every web host does.

Session replay, said plainly

Microsoft Clarity records how pages are used and lets us replay that as an anonymous session — where a reader scrolled, what they clicked, where they stopped.

We use it for one reason: to find out where a page is failing a reader, which a visit count cannot tell us. It is not tied to your name, and we are not watching anybody in particular. Text typed into the enquiry form is masked before it leaves your browser, so what you write in those fields does not appear in a recording. If you would rather not be recorded at all, the switch at the foot of this page turns it off along with everything else.

Enquiries

What happens when you contact us

You give us your name and email, and optionally a company or property, a telephone number, a city and a description of what is needed. We ask for nothing else, and you are free to leave the optional fields blank.

  1. The form is carried by Web3FormsA third-party service that takes the submission and emails it to us. It passes through their systems on the way. We do not use them to store a database of enquirers.
  2. It arrives in our email, run by Google WorkspaceFrom that point it sits in an ordinary mailbox, read by us, and it is subject to Google's handling of mail in transit and at rest.
  3. We use it to answer you, and for nothing elseNo list, no newsletter, no marketing sequence. We do not sell enquiry details, trade them, or pass them to anyone who is not working on your job.
  4. We keep it while it is usefulEnquiries that go nowhere are deleted within about two years. Correspondence tied to work we carried out is kept longer, because records of work performed have to be kept.

The client portal

What it holds, and who can see it

Houses in our estate care are given a private portal at portal.amalfibg.com. We open it, by invitation, for the people you name. There is no public sign-up, and nothing below applies to anyone who has only read this website.

  1. What is kept in itThe property, and notes about it. Photographs taken during our visits, each carrying the date and time it was taken. What was found, and what we propose doing about it. The prices we quote you. Your decision on each item, the name you type to sign it, and the moment you signed. Messages between you and the office. The visit times offered, and the one you chose.
  2. What we hold about you personallyA name, an email address, and a telephone number if you give one — for each person you ask us to add, commonly the owner and a house manager. Nothing further. We do not ask your date of birth, we hold no identity document, and we run no credit check.
  3. Who can see itOnly the people the office has added to your household, and the Amalfi staff working on your property. One client cannot reach another's, and that is enforced in the database itself rather than by hiding a screen. A crew member sees the job they have been assigned and nothing else. Nothing a technician records reaches you until the office has reviewed it.
  4. There is no passwordYou sign in with a link or a six-digit code sent to your email address, so there is no password for you to keep and none for us to lose. Our own administrator accounts carry a second step beyond that.
  5. No money moves through itThere is no payment screen and no card field. Invoicing is handled separately and sent to you by email. We hold no card or bank details for you, in the portal or anywhere else.

Where the portal is kept

Three companies sit behind it. Each is paid by us and bound to use what it holds for no purpose but running the portal.

Supabase holds the database, runs the sign-in and stores the photographs. Resend carries the portal's email — invitations, sign-in links and notices — sent from hello@amalfibg.com. Cloudflare serves the portal, and holds a copy of everything taken nightly into storage we control, so that a fault on our side cannot cost you a year of photographs. Each publishes its own terms, which govern what it does with what it receives.

Photographs and reports are kept for as long as we look after the house, and after we stop, because a record that is thrown away on the last day is not a record. If you would rather they were gone, write and say so, and they are deleted.

We do not photograph your property for our marketing, name you as a client, or discuss your house with anyone outside the firm. If we ever want to, we will ask you in writing, and you are free to say no.

What we never do

Shorter than the list above, and worth stating directly.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not run advertising or retargeting pixels on this site, so reading these pages does not cause our advertising to follow you elsewhere. We take no payment on this site or in the portal, and store no card or bank details anywhere. Portal accounts exist only by invitation, there is no public sign-up, and we hold no passwords for them. We do not knowingly collect anything from children, and nothing here is aimed at them.

If any of that changes — and if we ever advertise, it will — this page is changed first, with the date at the top updated.

Other services this page talks to

Loading any page here causes requests to a small number of third parties, each of which necessarily sees your network address.

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Google Fonts serves the two typefaces the pages are set in. Google and Microsoft receive the measurement described above, unless you switch it off. Web3Forms is contacted only at the moment you submit an enquiry, never before. Each of these publishes its own privacy terms, which govern what they do with what they receive.

Your choices

Whether or not a particular statute applies to a business of our size, these are available to anyone who asks.

Write to hello@amalfibg.com and ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. We will answer within thirty days and we will not make you explain why. If you have sent an enquiry and simply want it erased, say so and it is erased.

You can also clear or block cookies in your browser, and most browsers let you refuse third-party cookies entirely. Doing so does not stop you reading anything here.

Turn measurement off

This switch is kept in your own browser, on this device. While it is on, the measurement scripts are not loaded at all — not blocked after the fact, simply never fetched.
