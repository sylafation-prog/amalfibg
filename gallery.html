How the work is recorded

Photographed from the same place, every time

The three divisions do work that photographs badly and matters enormously — a coil you will never see, a terrace that falls correctly, a joint in the right place. We would rather show it properly than not at all.

The method

What a record actually consists of

Ten representative engagements, three or four to a division. Each describes a type of job, its typical setting and what the work actually consists of.

Fixed positions

Every property is photographed from a set of marked positions, and the same positions are used on every visit afterwards. Fifty pictures taken from wherever someone happened to stand prove that a person attended. Eleven taken from the same eleven places over three years show a hairline crack lengthening, a stain spreading, a tile lifting at one corner. Only the second is evidence of condition.

Before, during and after

Work is photographed before it starts, at the stage where the substrate is open and visible, and on completion. The middle one is the photograph that matters and the one almost nobody takes — what was behind the finish is the only record of why the specification was what it was.

The record belongs to the owner

Dated images, readings and exceptions go to the property file after every visit, through a private portal. It is the file an insurer reads after a storm, an adjuster reads on a claim, and a buyer’s inspector reads at sale — which is when the difference between a record and a promise becomes expensive.

Work on private houses is photographed to this standard and published only with the owner’s written permission, which is a slower way to build a portfolio and the only one compatible with the discretion these clients expect. We do not borrow other firms’ photographs, and we do not present a type of job as though it were a completed one. If you would like to see work in progress, ask and we will arrange it.

A note on photographs

Your property will not appear here

We do not photograph a client's property for marketing, name anyone as a client, or discuss a house or a building with anybody. If we ever want to show a job, we will ask in writing, and you may simply say no. Everything that eventually fills this page will have been agreed first.
